# Part4 Block3 — Cross-domain evidence-grounded intelligence

`analyzeCrossDomain` is a pure bounded read-only comparison over the existing Block2 trusted-server-supplied projections. It requires two to eight distinct declared domains and at least one source projection for each. The Block2 analyzer validates every supplied projection and workspace; unexpected domains, duplicate keys, absent domains, invalid provenance, and incompatible exact period boundaries fail closed. Source units and numerator/denominator stay attached to each individual metric: **no cross-unit arithmetic, causal inference, currency conversion or correlation-based recommendations**. Results preserve sorted fingerprint union and individual lineage, unverified status, insufficient confidence, and non-actionable ANALYSIS. Partial, stale and zero-denominator observations remain null with limitations. A displayed AVAILABLE status only denotes available arithmetic from declared projections, not independently verified business truth.

No provider adapter, external model, source verification authority, production authorization, durable storage, live Business OS integration, deployment or sensitive action execution is introduced. Part2/3 production gates and Block2 semantic source-validation limitations remain deferred.
