# Part8 Block1 — no-effect controlled-action policy preflight

The contract accepts a **pending** tenant-scoped Part7 Copilot review request and inert candidate intent (mode DEMO, SANDBOX or PRODUCTION). Every mode follows identical fail-closed checks and returns DENY_UNVERIFIED with zero effects, all six independent production gates UNSATISFIED, approved/actionable/executable/verifiedFact false, and explicit PENDING_REVIEW_IS_NOT_APPROVAL uncertainty. The preflight does not implement real policy authorization or human approval, does not authenticate source provenance, does not persist/replay requests, and does not call operational services or providers. Strings describing candidate operation and rationale are data, never interpreted as commands. Business OS retains operational truth/execution; future provider-neutral domain-service integration requires server-derived identity, actual approved/attested decisions, revocation, durable audit/idempotency and Demo/Sandbox parity. Part8 Block1 scoped in-memory foundation only, NOT production-ready. Scheduled CIGO automations disabled.
